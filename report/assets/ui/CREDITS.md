## What was rejected and why

Pexels and Unsplash have better fitness photography than anything used here
and both refuse automated access, so the search ran against Wikimedia Commons
and Openverse instead. That is the reason the pool was thin, and it is worth
recording: a later pass with a Pexels or Unsplash account would find a better
side-on bodyweight squat than the one in use.

No photograph was taken from an image search without a licence attached. The
temptation is real, because the best-looking results are always the ones with
no licence, and a report that ships an uncleared photograph has a defect that
no amount of good measurement makes up for.
